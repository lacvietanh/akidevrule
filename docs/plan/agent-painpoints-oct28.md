## Decision
The owner's order was to measure adherence per model and per receipt "trước khi vá thêm chữ". The first measurement found that among the clusters whose violated rule is decidable the rule was delivered and broken (44 COMPLY against 3 LOAD of 150 rows), that 103 of 150 rows cannot be decided because the extractor records compaction counts and not times, and that the regex behind the whole ranking has unmeasured recall. This plan therefore buys decidability and recall first, repeats the measurement on 2026-11-03 for the one rule change already made (`coding.B3`), and lets the result choose between a delivery fix, a mechanism, or no change. Text gaps shown by the evidence: none. Compliance-gap candidates: P01 (`agent.A4`) and P23 (`agent.B2`), the two clusters with a COMPLY plurality. The other 18 top-20 clusters are undecided, not cleared.
